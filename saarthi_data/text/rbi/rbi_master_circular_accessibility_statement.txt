Accessibility Statement Official website of Reserve Bank of India
(Version 2.2)
Reserve Bank of India is committed to ensuring digital accessibility to all, including people with
disabilities. To meet this commitment, the official website of Reserve Bank of India strives to be
accessible and has been tested by people with disabilities using various assistive technologies.
Using the website with various Assistive Technologies
Our goal is to ensure that this website is accessible to as many user groups as possible. The website
ensures:
• Navigating to the website pages using a keyboard or speech recognition software such
as Dragon Naturally Speaking.
• Navigating and operating the website by listening to it through screen readers such as
JAWS, NVDA, Narrator and VoiceOver.
• Accessing the website by using assistive technology such as ZoomText.
Accessibility of this website
General accessibility successes and areas of conformance
• All interactive elements are keyboard operable.
• No keyboard trap was found while navigating through the pages of the website using a
keyboard.
• Form controls have programmatically defined labels.
• Links have unique and descriptive text that clearly describes their destination target.
• When any component receives focus, it does not initiate a change of context.
• Sufficient color contrast is used for displaying the textual information.
• Plain text is used to present information rather than images of text.
Compliance status
This site conforms to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA issued by the Web
Accessibility Initiative (WAI) of the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), Indian Standard (IS) 17802
and Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW) 3.0.
Feedback and Contact Information
We appreciate any feedback, suggestions, questions or concerns about the accessibility of our
website. Please reach out to us at helpdoc@rbi.org.in.
The Accessibility Statement is dated November 28, 2025.
